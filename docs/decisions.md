- 2026-10-06: Real architectural finding from reading `@x402/next`'s actual source (`dist/esm/index.js`), not just its README: `withX402` runs the wrapped route handler FIRST, then settles payment AFTER by mutating the handler's own response with settlement headers (`handleSettlement()`).
